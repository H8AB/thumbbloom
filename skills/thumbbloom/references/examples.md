# Example briefs

These are fictional demonstrations; names and claims are illustrative.

1. **Video** — A creator explains a five-minute desk reset routine for people who work from home. Audience: busy remote workers. Direction: bold and energetic. Suggested cover copy: `5 分钟 / 重启桌面`.
2. **Article** — A long-form piece explains how to plan a personal knowledge system with paper and digital notes. Audience: thoughtful beginners. Direction: editorial. Suggested cover copy: `把知识 / 整理成系统`.
3. **Podcast** — A conversation about small creative habits that survive a busy schedule. Audience: creators. Direction: minimal. Suggested cover copy: `每天一点 / 也算创作`.

For each brief, produce at least two concept directions, choose one, render an editable cover, and inspect it at small size. The repository's `examples/` folder contains rendered SVG demonstrations from the same shared browser renderer.
