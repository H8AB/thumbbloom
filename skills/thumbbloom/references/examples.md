# Example briefs

These are fictional demonstrations; names and claims are illustrative.

1. **Video** — A creator explains a five-minute desk reset routine for people who work from home. Audience: busy remote workers. Direction: bold and energetic. Suggested cover copy: `5-MINUTE / DESK RESET`.
2. **Article** — A long-form piece explains how to plan a personal knowledge system with paper and digital notes. Audience: thoughtful beginners. Direction: editorial. Suggested cover copy: `BUILD A / NOTE SYSTEM`.
3. **Podcast** — A conversation about small creative habits that survive a busy schedule. Audience: creators. Direction: minimal. Suggested cover copy: `CREATE / EVERY DAY`.

For each brief, produce at least two concept directions, choose one, render an editable cover, and inspect it at small size. The repository's `examples/` folder contains rendered SVG demonstrations from the included renderer.
